About

Mango Letters was born from a fascination with hand-painted signage — the imperfect, warm kind you'd find at a tropical fruit stand. Every letter has its own personality.

Designed to be warm, playful, and unmistakably human. Works beautifully for branding, packaging, posters, social media, and anywhere you need handcrafted charm.

Includes uppercase, lowercase, numbers, punctuation, and a full set of alternates — all carefully spaced and kerned.
