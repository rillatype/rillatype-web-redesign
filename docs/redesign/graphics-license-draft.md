## Definitions

**Asset** means the illustration, texture, overlay, or other graphic files included in the licensed Rillatype product.

**End Design** means a finished design that incorporates the Asset, such as a package design, advertisement, social media image, or merchandise design.

**Licensee** means the buyer identified by the order. The permitted scope for employees and other authorized users is to be confirmed before publication.

These licenses grant permission to use the Asset. They do not transfer ownership of the original Asset or grant exclusive rights to it.

## Graphics Standard

You may use the Asset in personal projects and commercial End Designs, including:

- Brand presentations and visual materials.
- Product packaging and labels.
- Advertising, promotional materials, and social media content.
- Finished designs created for clients.

You may edit the Asset as part of your End Design. Editing the Asset does not give you permission to redistribute it as an asset file.

Graphics Standard does not cover merchandise for sale or print-on-demand products. These uses require Graphics Extended.

When a product offers Graphics Standard for free, the same Graphics Standard permissions and restrictions apply. A free price does not grant Graphics Extended rights.

## Graphics Extended

Graphics Extended includes all Graphics Standard permissions and additionally permits:

- Physical merchandise for sale, such as clothing, mugs, posters, and stationery incorporating the Asset.
- Print-on-demand products incorporating the Asset.
- An unlimited number of physical copies of the permitted merchandise.

An unlimited print quantity does not permit selling or distributing the Asset files themselves. Graphics Extended is a paid option, including when Graphics Standard is free.

Digital editable templates and other uses that make the Asset available as a reusable file are not granted by the permissions in this draft. Such a product cannot be treated as ordinary physical merchandise without a separate decision.

## Client work

You may provide a client with the finished End Design for its intended use within your license permissions. For merchandise and print-on-demand work, Graphics Extended is required.

A client needs a separate license if the client receives the original Asset files or wants to use the Asset in other projects. Your license does not authorize handing the original Asset to a client for independent reuse.

Delivering a finished design does not give the client ownership or exclusive rights to the original Asset.

## Restrictions for both licenses

You may not:

- Sell, share, sublicense, or distribute the original Asset files as graphic assets.
- Repackage the Asset as an illustration collection, texture pack, stock resource, or other reusable asset library.
- Offer the Asset files as a free download, even when you obtained Graphics Standard for free.
- Claim ownership or exclusive rights to the original Asset.
- Transfer the original Asset files to a client for independent reuse without that client's own license.

These restrictions also apply to modified Asset files when they are offered as reusable graphics rather than as a finished End Design.
